ScrollCount Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

ScrollCount helps you see how many Reels and Shorts you watch each day. It works entirely on your phone.

Data we collect

None. ScrollCount has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. It cannot send any data off your device, and we never receive any information about you.

What stays on your phone

  • Daily totals: time spent in Instagram, YouTube and Facebook, and the estimated number of short videos watched in each.
  • Your settings: tracked apps, daily limit, theme and counter icon.

This is stored only in the app's private storage and is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

Usage Access

With your permission, ScrollCount uses Android's Usage Access to read how long the apps you choose were open. It does not see what you did inside them.

Accessibility Service

ScrollCount offers an optional swipe counter that uses Android's Accessibility Service. It is not an accessibility tool for people with disabilities. Before you turn it on, the app explains what it does and asks for your consent.

When on, it only looks at which app is open and the layout of its video feed, to tell when you swipe to a new video, and to show a small counter on screen. It never reads text, messages or passwords, never takes screenshots, and never stores or shares anything except the counts above.

You can turn it off any time in Settings → Accessibility → ScrollCount.

Deleting your data

Tap Clear all local data in the app's settings, or uninstall the app. Both permanently delete everything it stored.

Children

ScrollCount is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
